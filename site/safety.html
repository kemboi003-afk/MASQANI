House hunt with confidence.

Verification supports your decision; it never replaces an in-person viewing and your own checks.

Before you view

Compare the information

Check rent, type, photo and location are consistent. Ask clear questions about water, electricity, deposit and service charges.

Arrange a safe viewing

Meet during daylight, tell someone where you are going and do not rely on a photo alone.

Protect your money

Do not send deposits or “booking fees” before seeing the property and confirming who you are dealing with.

What Masqani Verified means

A verified badge may only be added after MASQANI reviews the landlord identity and listing information. It does not mean MASQANI owns the property or guarantees a tenancy.

The current public preview cards are explicitly not verified. Report anything suspicious through your account once reporting is activated.

Think a listing is false?

Save screenshots, stop communicating if you feel unsafe, and report the listing. Do not accuse a person publicly; submit the facts for review.
